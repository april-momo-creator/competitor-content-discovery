# 02 Competitor Candidate Discovery

## Use When

Use when the user needs to know who should be studied, or when the competitor list is missing, too narrow, or based only on intuition.

## Goal

Build a broad but useful candidate pool before judging competitor type or priority.

## Prerequisite

Product intake and initial keywords, or an equivalent product brief.

## Discovery Clues

| Clue | Where To Search | What To Record |
|---|---|---|
| Official comparison and SEO pages | Product site footer, comparison, alternatives, vs, pricing, FAQ | Brands the product or competitors actively compare against |
| Search and third-party lists | Google/Bing, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, Zapier-style lists | Repeated mainstream players |
| Social attention pool | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn, X | Brands or accounts capturing the target audience's attention |
| Reviews and community complaints | Reddit, G2/Capterra reviews, Product Hunt comments, app/extension stores, YouTube/TikTok comments | Substitute paths, complaints, purchase concerns |
| Aspirational persona fit | Products loved by the same target users | Mature content/UGC examples worth learning from |

## Steps

1. Search initial category, pain, scenario, persona, competitor, and alternative terms.
2. Record every plausible candidate once, even if the type is unclear.
3. Tag the evidence line that surfaced each candidate.
4. Avoid judging too early; this scenario only creates the candidate pool.

## Deliverable

| Candidate | URL | Evidence Line | Source Link | Initial Type Guess | Problem Solved | User Overlap | Why It May Matter | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Initial Type Guess values: direct, indirect, substitute, aspirational/content, unknown.
