# 06 Owned Social Map

## Use When

Use when the user asks how competitors operate on social, or when a Social Search Map needs competitor-owned content inputs.

## Goal

Understand how competitors, as brands, educate the market and validate content directions on official channels.

## Platforms

Prioritize platforms based on product type. For social/UGC/video research, start with TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, X, and YouTube. For review/objection signals, use Reddit, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, app stores, extension stores, and comments.

## Steps

1. Find official handles from the website, search, or platform search.
2. Record platform presence and visible account metrics.
3. Inspect bio, pinned/featured content, recent cadence, and high-performing posts.
4. Capture common formats, CTAs, product exposure style, UGC/creator/employee signals, and comment feedback.
5. Mark learn/avoid notes for later search-map generation.

## Deliverable

| Competitor | Platform | Official Handle/URL | Followers | Update Frequency | Bio/Positioning | Pinned/Featured Content | Recent Focus | High-Performing Content | Formats | UGC/Creator Signal | CTA | Comment Feedback | Learn/Avoid |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

If official accounts are weak or absent, record that and rely more on content candidate collection from UGC, reviews, and community sources.
