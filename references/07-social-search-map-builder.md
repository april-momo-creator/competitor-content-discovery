# 07 Social Search Map Builder

## Use When

Use before collecting content candidates. This scenario turns product, competitor, positioning, differentiation, and owned-social observations into platform-specific search instructions.

## Goal

Avoid random content browsing by creating a precise map of what to search, where to search, and why.

## Inputs

- Product intake and initial keywords.
- Priority competitor pool.
- Competitor positioning cards.
- Differentiation hypothesis matrix.
- Owned social observations when available.

## Keyword Groups

| Group | Example Terms | Platforms | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor brand | [competitor] review, [competitor] tutorial, [competitor] alternative | YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, G2 | Find reviews, tutorials, objections |
| Category | best [category] tools, AI [category] app | Google, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit | Understand category framing |
| Scenario | [scenario] tool, how to [scenario task] | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Reddit | Find scenario-native content |
| Persona | tools for [persona], [persona] productivity tips | TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube | Find target-user language |
| Pain | I hate [pain], too much [problem], how to stop [pain] | TikTok, Reddit, X, Reels | Find emotional hooks |
| Substitute path | best way to [task], [task] without [category] | Reddit, YouTube, X, Google | Find indirect alternatives |
| Differentiation | [product opportunity] tool, how to [desired result] | Google, TikTok, YouTube | Test product-specific angles |
| Community/hashtag | #[vertical]Tok, #[category]Tips, #[persona]Life | TikTok, Reels | Find platform-native language |
