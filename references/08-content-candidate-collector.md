# 08 Content Candidate Collector

## Use When

Use after a Social Search Map exists and the user asks to collect competitor content, user content, creator content, review/community content, or raw references for later analysis.

## Goal

Create a raw content candidate pool. Do not perform deep content analysis, winning/losing synthesis, opportunity-gap synthesis, or final reference-pool curation in this scenario.

## Search Order

1. Competitor product names.
2. Category, scenario, and pain terms.
3. Persona terms.
4. Substitute-path terms.
5. Differentiation terms.
6. Community hashtags and comment language.

## Collection Rules

- Record why each item was collected.
- Do not over-collect irrelevant viral content.
- For social posts, capture visible metrics and observation date.
- For comments/reviews, capture the user question, objection, purchase signal, or substitute mention.
- Keep direct competitors as the main source; use indirect competitors for substitute/pain evidence; use aspirational competitors for content format evidence.

## Suggested Volume

For a full pass, collect 80-150 raw candidates. For a lightweight pass, collect 20-40. Adjust based on evidence availability.

## Deliverable

| ID | Candidate Type | Source | Platform | Account | Link | Date Observed | Visible Metrics | Followers | Topic | Search Term Used | Why Collected | Related Competitor/Keyword | Initial Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Candidate Type values: competitor official, competitor UGC, creator review, tutorial, alternative/comparison, community complaint, review insight, substitute-path content, aspirational format, weak/avoid sample.
