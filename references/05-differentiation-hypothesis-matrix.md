# 05 Differentiation Hypothesis Matrix

## Use When

Use after product intake and competitor positioning cards. This scenario should happen before social search-map generation.

## Goal

Turn product-vs-competitor differences into content-ready hypotheses: what to emphasize, what to counter, what to avoid, and what to test.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Function | What does the product do better, simpler, faster, or more completely? |
| Scenario | Which use cases are more natural for the product than competitors? |
| Persona | Which users are better served by the product? |
| Pain | Which pain is more specific, frequent, or emotionally sharp? |
| Experience | Is the product easier, lighter, less disruptive, more automatic, or lower-friction? |
| Result | What outcome does the user get: speed, clarity, quality, confidence, cost saving, control? |
| Emotion | What feeling can content own: relief, control, confidence, professionalism, calm? |
| Content | What are competitors overusing, under-explaining, or failing to show? |

## Steps

1. Compare the product against each priority competitor by dimension.
2. Convert each difference into a content angle, not just a feature note.
3. Attach evidence and confidence level.
4. Feed strongest hypotheses into the Social Search Map.

## Deliverable

| Dimension | Competitor Pattern | Product Opportunity | Contentizable Angle | Search Terms To Test | Confidence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Confidence values: high, medium, low, needs validation.
